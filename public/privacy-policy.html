Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Pacmonk ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Pacmonk mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our service to you:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, company name
  • Business Information: GST number, registered business address (optional)
  • Delivery Information: Delivery addresses you save for order fulfillment
  • Order Information: Products ordered, quantities, specifications, artwork files
  • Payment Information: Payment method preferences (actual payment details are processed by third-party providers)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Pages viewed, time spent on the App, crash reports
  • Location Data: We may use location services for delivery address suggestions (with your permission)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Process and fulfill your packaging material orders
  • Send order status updates via push notifications
  • Communicate with you about your orders via WhatsApp, SMS, or email
  • Generate GST-compliant invoices
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Improve our App functionality and user experience
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) with the following security measures:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Data at rest is encrypted on Firebase servers
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • We use Firebase Authentication for secure user sessions
  • Regular security audits and updates are performed

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share data with trusted third parties who assist in order fulfillment (printing, shipping, payment processing)
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests
  • Business Transfers: If we are involved in a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction

5. Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, file storage, analytics, crash reporting
  • Payment Processors: For secure payment processing (we do not store card details)
  • WhatsApp Business API: For order notifications and customer communication

6. Push Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications about order status updates. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Order history is retained for 7 years to comply with tax and accounting regulations.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where consent was the legal basis

9. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside of India where Firebase servers are located. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Last updated" date.

12. Google Play Data Safety

This App complies with Google Play's Data Safety requirements:

  • Data collected: Personal info, Financial info, Photos/videos, Files, Location, App activity, Device identifiers
  • Data usage: App functionality, Analytics, Fraud prevention, Personalization, Account management
  • Data sharing: Service providers for order fulfillment only
  • Security: Data encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Deletion: Users can request data deletion by contacting support
